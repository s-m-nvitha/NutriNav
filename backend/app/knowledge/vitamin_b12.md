# Vitamin B12 Deficiency

Vitamin B12 is essential for:

- Red blood cell formation
- Nerve function
- Energy production
- Brain health

Common symptoms:

- Fatigue
- Weakness
- Memory problems
- Tingling in hands and feet
- Dizziness

Vegetarian Sources:

- Fortified cereals
- Nutritional yeast
- Fortified plant milk
- Fortified soy products

Recommendations:

- Include fortified foods daily
- Monitor Vitamin B12 levels regularly
- Follow medical advice for supplementation if needed

Important:

Severe Vitamin B12 deficiency may require supplementation under medical supervision.
