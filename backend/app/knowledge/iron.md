# Iron Deficiency

Iron helps produce hemoglobin which carries oxygen throughout the body.

Common Symptoms:
- Fatigue
- Weakness
- Pale skin
- Dizziness
- Shortness of breath

Iron Rich Foods:
- Spinach
- Lentils
- Chickpeas
- Beans
- Pumpkin Seeds
- Tofu

Iron Absorption Tips:
- Pair iron foods with Vitamin C
- Eat oranges, lemons, strawberries, bell peppers
- Avoid tea and coffee immediately after meals

Daily Recommendation:
Include at least one iron-rich food in every meal.
