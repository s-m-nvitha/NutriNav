# Diabetes Nutrition Guide

Goals:

- Maintain stable blood sugar
- Prevent spikes and crashes
- Improve long-term health

Recommended Foods:

- Whole grains
- Oats
- Brown rice
- Quinoa
- Vegetables
- Lentils
- Beans
- Nuts
- Seeds

Foods to Limit:

- Sugary drinks
- Sweets
- Refined carbohydrates
- Highly processed foods

Healthy Habits:

- Eat regular meals
- Include protein with meals
- Stay hydrated
- Exercise regularly

Blood Sugar Friendly Snacks:

- Nuts
- Apple slices
- Roasted chickpeas
- Unsweetened yogurt alternatives
