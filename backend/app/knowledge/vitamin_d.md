# Vitamin D Deficiency

Vitamin D supports:

- Bone health
- Immune function
- Muscle strength
- Calcium absorption

Common symptoms:

- Fatigue
- Bone pain
- Muscle weakness
- Frequent illness

Food Sources:

- UV-exposed mushrooms
- Fortified cereals
- Fortified plant milk
- Fortified foods

Lifestyle Tips:

- Safe sunlight exposure
- Regular physical activity
- Balanced nutrition

Recommendations:

- Include fortified foods regularly
- Monitor Vitamin D levels
- Follow healthcare guidance for supplementation

Important:

Very low Vitamin D levels often require supplementation.
