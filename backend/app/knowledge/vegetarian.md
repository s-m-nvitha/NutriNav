# Vegetarian Nutrition Guide

A healthy vegetarian diet should include:

Protein Sources:

- Lentils
- Chickpeas
- Beans
- Tofu
- Tempeh
- Soy products

Iron Sources:

- Spinach
- Lentils
- Beans
- Pumpkin seeds
- Sesame seeds

Vitamin B12 Sources:

- Fortified cereals
- Nutritional yeast
- Fortified plant milk

Healthy Fats:

- Nuts
- Seeds
- Avocados

Whole Grains:

- Oats
- Brown rice
- Whole wheat products
- Quinoa

Tips:

- Pair iron-rich foods with Vitamin C sources
- Include protein in every meal
- Eat a variety of fruits and vegetables
- Monitor nutrient deficiencies regularly
